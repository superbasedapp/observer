# Sign in and connect

SuperBased Observer is already running on this machine and capturing your
AI sessions. Sign in to see all your machines online and join your team.

**SuperBased: Sign in and connect** opens a page in your browser. There you
pick where this machine shows up and see what will be shared before
anything is sent:

| Where | What is shared |
|---|---|
| **Personal dashboard** (made for you on first sign-in) | Summary only. No prompts, responses or file paths. |
| **A team** | Full detail: prompts, responses and file paths. Team admins can read it. |

Choose **Not now** on that page to stay signed in without sending anything.

The editor shows a short code while it waits, like `KTRW-4821`. If no
browser opens (a remote machine, for example), open the connect page on
any device and enter that code.

Joining a team? Run **SuperBased: Connect to a team...** and enter the team
name your admin gave you.

The status bar shows **SuperBased · not signed in** until you connect, then
the name of your dashboard or team. Click it to open that dashboard.

### No account needed

Everything else in this walkthrough works locally with no account. You can
sign in later from the status bar or the command palette.
